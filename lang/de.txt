In the toolbar layout editor, find BatteryHub and add it to the section you want.\n\nStep 3 - Done\nBattery levels for devices detected by BatteryHub should now appear in the toolbar. The integration is already enabled; you can change it later from the BatteryHub menu.\n\nDetailed documentation:\n = Schritt 1 – Plugin installieren\nÖffne die BatteryHub-Plugin-Seite in Seelen UI und installiere es.
